Why OptiVox Exists

Surveillance should understand, not just record.

Most camera systems are passive — they capture footage nobody watches until after something has already gone wrong. OptiVox was built to close that gap.

MISSION

Make every camera intelligent

Turn ordinary cameras into a perceptive system that recognizes people, detects threats, tracks attendance, and reasons about behavior — in real time, on the edge.

VISION

Security that respects privacy

Intelligence shouldn't require surrendering your data to the cloud. OptiVox runs locally so faces, footage, and analytics stay inside your walls by default.

The Problem

Why traditional systems fall short

Reactive, not proactive

Footage is reviewed after incidents. By then the moment to intervene has passed.

Human-bandwidth limited

No operator can meaningfully watch dozens of feeds for hours without missing events.

Cloud dependency

Many "smart" systems stream sensitive video off-site, raising cost, latency, and privacy risk.

Ethics & Responsible Use

Powerful tools demand clear boundaries

Facial recognition and behavioral analysis carry real privacy and civil-liberties implications.
